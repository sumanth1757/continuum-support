Continuum: Customer Campaigns

Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026. Sumanth Karasala operates Continuum. For privacy questions, email info@aximary.com.

Current service status

The public Continuum server is being prepared and is not available yet. The app can show a fictional sample workspace when connected to a compatible server. Sandbox campaign results are simulated; the current release does not send live marketing email or call an external AI provider.

Data in a workspace

When you create an account on a Continuum server, it stores the account name and email you provide, a password hash, and sessions. A workspace may contain imported customer names and emails, purchase history, marketing consent choices, events, campaigns, brand notes, styles, approvals, and activity records. The server uses these records to sign you in, show your workspace, calculate audiences, enforce consent and approvals, and save campaign results. Use fictional records in a sample workspace.

If you choose a server operated by someone else, that server operator controls the records you send there. Check its privacy practices before connecting or importing customer data.

Storage and sharing

The iOS app stores its session using iOS security features. A browser session uses a cookie. The current clients contain no advertising tracker or analytics SDK. We do not sell workspace data. The planned public Continuum service will use Render in Ohio, United States, for hosting and persistent storage. We will update this page with its verified storage and backup details before accepting real customer data on that service.

Deletion and retention

A workspace owner can delete their account and workspace from Settings on the web or iOS app. A real account must confirm with its current password. The application then removes live workspace records and sessions. Data may remain in server backups until their retention period ends; the operator of a server you select controls its backups. For account deletion help, contact info@aximary.com. Do not send passwords or customer records by email.

Updates

We will update this policy when the public service or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
